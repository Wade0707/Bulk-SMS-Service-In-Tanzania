## Customer Segmentation

A large customer database does not always need to receive the same message.

Businesses can create groups based on:

* Customer location
* Product interest
* Purchase history
* Customer type
* Membership status
* Recent activity
* Previous interactions

For example, a retail business could send one promotion to customers interested in electronics and another to customers interested in clothing.

## SMS Campaign Planning

Before launching a campaign, businesses can define a few basic elements.

### Campaign Objective

Decide what the SMS campaign should communicate.

### Target Audience

Identify the customer group that should receive the message.

### Message Content

### Campaign Timing

Choose a suitable time based on the campaign type and audience.

This simple process can make SMS campaigns more consistent.

Businesses can review these reports to identify delivery issues and improve future messaging workflows.

## Security and Responsible Messaging

Customer contact information and SMS credentials should be handled carefully.

Important practices include:

* Protect API credentials.
* Restrict access to customer databases.
* Avoid storing sensitive keys in public repositories.
* Review recipient lists before campaigns.
* Avoid unnecessary exposure of personal information.
* Monitor unusual messaging activity.
* Follow applicable messaging and privacy requirements.

Responsible communication also means keeping messages relevant and avoiding unnecessary contact.
